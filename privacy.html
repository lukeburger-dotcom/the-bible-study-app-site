Privacy Policy

The Bible Study App · Effective October 27, 2026

The short version: there are no accounts. Your study history lives only on your device, except that, if you start a plan from someone's link, the days you finish are counted for your group, without your name or device ID, unless you turn it off. When you ask for a study or devotional, your question is sent to our server and processed by Anthropic's Claude to write the response. We identify your device with a random ID, never your name, email, or Apple ID. If you lead a group and choose to give your first name when you share a plan, it is shown to the people you share it with. If you lead a group, notes you send it are stored with the group. If you ask your group's leader a question, it is stored on our server without your device ID, with your first name only if you add it, and deleted from our server 60 days after the day it was sent, and from our rolling backups within 8 days after that. We don't sell data, and we don't run ads.

What we collect and why

Your study questions and conversation. When you request a study, devotional, or follow-up, the text you enter (and the conversation thread it belongs to) is sent to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the response. Per Anthropic's API terms, this data is not used to train their models. We do not permanently store the content of your studies on our server, except what you choose to share: a study or plan you share by link is stored so the people you send it to can open it.

A random device identifier. Your device generates a random ID on first launch. It accompanies requests so we can count free studies, apply fair-use rate limits, and check subscription status. It is not derived from and cannot be linked to your name, email, phone number, or Apple ID by us.

Group progress. In a plan you started from someone's link, your phone sends the link, the number of each day you finish, and a random number our server made for your copy of the plan (not your device ID). We keep them with the date you started, not the time. The person who shared the plan sees only counts: how many are counted, how many are keeping up, and the days done together, and nothing at all while fewer than 3 people are counted. Turn it off in the plan's menu ("Count me in my group's progress"); that deletes what was sent.

A group leader's first name (optional). When you share a group plan you may give your first name. It is stored with that shared plan on our server and shown to anyone who opens its link, on the invite page and in the app. It is never sent to Anthropic. Leave it empty to share without one; change it for your next share in the plan's settings. If someone reports a leader's words, the name is hidden with them.

Group notes and questions. A group leader can send notes to the people in their group: a note is stored on our server with the group and shown to its members in the app until the leader deletes it. A member can send their group's leader a question, with a first name if they choose. A question is stored on our server without your device ID or any account, only the leader sees it, and it is deleted from our server 60 days after the day it was sent, and from our rolling backups within 8 days after that. Neither is ever sent to Anthropic. Links, contact details and offensive words are refused. A reported note stays hidden from the group's members; if its leader deletes it, we keep it for our review for 60 days after the report; a question the leader reports is kept for our review until its 60 days are up.

Purchase status. Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to know whether your device's random ID has an active subscription. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

Your study history, saved conversations, settings, and translation preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. One exception: in a plan started from someone's link, the days you finish are counted for your group, as described above, unless you turn it off. If you gave a first name for sharing, your device remembers it for your next share. Which notes you hid or reported, which questions you have seen as a leader, and that you agreed to the terms for notes or questions, are kept only on your device.

What we don't do

No selling or sharing of personal data. No advertising or ad tracking. No third-party analytics SDKs. No profiles. If any of this ever changes, this policy will change first and the app will say so plainly.

AI processing consent

The app asks for your consent before your first study, and you can withdraw it anytime in Settings → Privacy. With consent off, no text leaves your device and new studies are paused.

Data retention

Free-study counts and subscription status tied to the random device ID are kept for as long as needed to operate the service. You can request deletion of server-side records for your device ID by emailing us.

Group progress is kept until you turn counting off. It is not tied to your device ID, so a deletion request by device ID cannot find it; turning counting off deletes it from our server, and from our rolling backups within 8 days after that.

A leader's first name is kept on our server with the shared plan it came with (and that plan's earlier versions) until you ask us to delete it; there is no way to unshare a plan in the app. The server keeps a key derived from the sharing device's ID beside each share, so if you email us a deletion request with your device ID, we can find and remove your shares and the name with them.

A question to a group leader is deleted from our server 60 days after the day it was sent, and from our rolling backups within 8 days after that. It carries no device ID, so a deletion request by device ID cannot find it. A leader's notes are kept with the group until the leader deletes them or asks us to; a reported note the leader deletes is kept for our review for 60 days after the report. The server can find a leader's groups from the key it keeps for the leader's device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new effective date. Meaningful changes will be noted in the app.
